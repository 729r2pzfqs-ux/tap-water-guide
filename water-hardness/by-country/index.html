Water Hardness by Country: Complete Guide

Water hardness varies dramatically from country to country, driven almost entirely by local geology. Countries drawing water from granite and volcanic rock — like Iceland and Norway — have very soft water, while those sitting on limestone and chalk — like Malta and Cyprus — have very hard water. The table below covers every country on this site, with typical hardness levels and classifications.
