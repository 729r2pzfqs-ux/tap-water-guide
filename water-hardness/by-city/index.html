Water Hardness by City: Complete Guide

Tap water hardness varies more between cities than almost any other water-quality measure — from under 30 mg/L in Seattle to well over 300 mg/L in Las Vegas and Rome. Every city on this site is listed below with its typical hardness level and mg/L range, grouped by the same four-band USGS scale used on every city page.
