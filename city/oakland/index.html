Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Oakland?

Yes, tap water in Oakland is safe to drink and is among the best urban water in California. EBMUD delivers protected Sierra snowmelt from the Mokelumne River, and it meets all EPA and state standards.

Water Source

The East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) supplies Oakland with Sierra Nevada snowmelt from the Mokelumne River, stored at Pardee Reservoir in the foothills and piped nearly 90 miles to the East Bay, supplemented by protected local reservoirs.

EPA Compliance Status

Meets all federal Safe Drinking Water Act standards and California's stricter state requirements. EBMUD's protected mountain source consistently produces some of the cleanest big-city source water in the state.

Notable Contaminants & Context

  • •No significant source-water concerns; the Mokelumne watershed is largely protected and lightly developed
  • •Some pre-1950s East Bay homes have galvanized or lead-containing plumbing; EBMUD runs corrosion control and a service line inventory under the EPA lead rule
  • •Trace disinfection byproducts are monitored and well within limits

How It Compares

Oakland's Mokelumne supply is often compared to San Francisco's Hetch Hetchy: both are protected Sierra snowmelt systems that outclass most U.S. urban sources for natural purity.

Tips

  • •Oakland tap water is excellent unfiltered; chilling it removes most chlorine taste
  • •Check EBMUD's online service line lookup if you live in a pre-1950s home
  • •EBMUD publishes detailed annual water quality reports
