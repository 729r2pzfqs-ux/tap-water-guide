Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Dubai?

Yes, tap water in Dubai is technically safe to drink, produced by large-scale seawater desalination and treated to strict standards, though most residents and hotels default to bottled water for taste.

Hardness
Low to moderate

United Arab Emirates
Rating
Generally Safe

Water Source

Dubai's tap water comes almost entirely from seawater desalination plants along the Gulf coast, treated and remineralized before distribution.

Contaminants & Concerns

No significant contamination risk at the treatment stage. Water quality can be affected by poorly maintained rooftop storage tanks in some older buildings before it reaches individual units.

Tips

  • •Bottled water is extremely cheap and the strong cultural default
  • •In new towers and hotels, tap water is generally fine
  • •If staying long-term, ask your building about rooftop tank cleaning schedules
