Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Seoul?

Yes, tap water in Seoul (arisu) is treated to a safe standard and meets government and WHO quality guidelines, though many residents still filter or boil it out of habit.

Hardness
Soft to moderate
Country
South Korea
Rating
Safe

Water Source

Seoul's water authority treats water from the Han River to government standards regulated by the Ministry of Environment, branding the finished product "Arisu."

Contaminants & Concerns

No significant health concerns in treated supply. Some older apartment buildings have aging internal plumbing or rooftop storage tanks that can affect taste.

Tips

  • •Public water fountains, particularly in subway stations and parks, are safe to drink from
  • •Many households use a purifier or boil water purely by habit, not necessity
  • •Bottled water is widely available if preferred
