Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Buenos Aires?

Yes, tap water in Buenos Aires is safe to drink, treated by AySA from the Río de la Plata with modern filtration and disinfection.

Water Source

AySA treats water drawn from the Río de la Plata at large modern treatment plants serving the greater Buenos Aires metro area.

Contaminants & Concerns

No significant health concerns in the municipal system.
