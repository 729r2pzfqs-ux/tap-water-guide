Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Mykonos?

No, tap water on Mykonos is not recommended for drinking. The island depends on desalination plants, and the water often has a salty or mineral taste with variable quality at the point of use.

Water Source

Mykonos relies on desalination plants to produce freshwater, supplemented by limited groundwater extraction. Like most Cycladic islands, it has no significant natural freshwater sources.

Contaminants & Concerns

No dangerous contamination from treatment, but residual salinity, high mineral levels, and variable building-level storage conditions can affect tap water quality.

Tips

  • •Bottled water is the standard for drinking on Mykonos
  • •Hotels and restaurants provide bottled water as the default
  • •Tap water is fine for showering and brushing teeth
