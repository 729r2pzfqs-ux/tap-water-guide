Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Munich?

Yes, tap water in Munich is safe to drink and consistently ranked among the best in Germany, sourced from protected Alpine foothill springs by Stadtwerke München.

Water Source

Stadtwerke München (SWM) supplies Munich primarily from springs and groundwater in the Mangfall Valley and the protected Alpine foothills south of the city. The naturally filtered mountain water requires only UV treatment before distribution.

Contaminants & Concerns

No significant health concerns. The protected spring catchment areas have been safeguarded since the late 19th century, ensuring consistently clean source water.
