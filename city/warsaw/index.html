Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Warsaw?

Yes, tap water in Warsaw is safe to drink, supplied by MPWiK Warszawa from the Vistula River with modern multi-stage treatment meeting EU drinking water standards.

Hardness
Moderate

Poland

Safe

Water Source

MPWiK Warszawa (Miejskie Przedsiębiorstwo Wodociągów i Kanalizacji) treats Vistula River water at modern treatment plants using ozonation, activated carbon filtration, and UV disinfection.

Contaminants & Concerns

No significant health concerns. The water undergoes advanced multi-stage treatment and meets all EU Drinking Water Directive parameters.

Tips

  • •Warsaw tap water has improved dramatically in recent decades and is now safe to drink directly
  • •In older pre-war buildings, run the tap briefly before drinking as a precaution for internal plumbing
  • •No need to buy bottled water in restaurants — ask for woda z kranu (tap water)
