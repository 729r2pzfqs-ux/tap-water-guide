Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Estonia?

Yes, tap water in Estonia is safe to drink and meets EU Drinking Water Directive standards. Tallinna Vesi reports a 99.5% compliance rate across more than 3,000 annual water samples.

Water Source

Tallinn is supplied by AS Tallinna Vesi from Lake Ülemiste, pumping approximately 70,000 cubic meters daily. The utility is ISO-certified with a 14% leakage rate, among the best in the EU. Tartu uses AS Tartu Veevärk with artesian groundwater.

Contaminants & Concerns

The Cambrian-Vendian aquifer in northern Estonia contains naturally occurring radium isotopes, affecting about 19% of the population. Treatment is being implemented. Tallinn's primary source (Lake Ülemiste) is surface water unaffected by this.

Regional Variations

Tallinn has excellent quality from Lake Ülemiste with 99.5% compliance. Tartu uses artesian groundwater with higher mineral content. Rural areas with private wells have variable quality.

Water Hardness

Varies seasonally in Tallinn: roughly 250 mg/L in winter, 180 mg/L in summer. Tartu groundwater tends harder. Southern Estonia has softer groundwater..

Do Locals Drink Tap Water?

Yes, approximately 90% of Tallinn residents drink tap water. It is the norm in homes, workplaces, and restaurants.

Tips for Travelers

  • •Tap water is safe to drink throughout Estonia's cities — Tallinn, Tartu, and Pärnu all have EU-standard treated supply
  • •If you notice chlorine taste, let water sit in a jug briefly or chill in the refrigerator
  • •Tallinn operates free public drinking water taps during warmer months

Recommended Precautions

In rural areas with private wells, verify water quality with the property owner before drinking
