Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Colombia?

Tap water in Bogotá is officially rated safe to drink by the city's water utility, but most other Colombian cities and rural areas are not reliably safe, so bottled water is recommended outside the capital.

Water Source

Bogotá's utility, Empresa de Acueducto de Bogotá, draws from high-altitude páramo watersheds and is widely regarded as some of the best-treated municipal water in Latin America. Other cities have varying levels of treatment.

Contaminants & Concerns

Bogotá water tests clean; cities like Cartagena, Medellín, and coastal/rural areas have more inconsistent treatment and distribution issues, especially after heavy rain.

Regional Variations

Safe in Bogotá. Use bottled or filtered water in Cartagena, Santa Marta, rural coffee region towns, and the Amazon/Pacific coastal areas.

Water Hardness

Soft, roughly 30–80 mg/L in Bogotá due to mountain páramo sourcing..

Do Locals Drink Tap Water?

Yes in Bogotá; in much of the rest of the country, bottled or boiled water is more common.

Tips for Travelers

  • •Bogotá is a rare Latin American capital where tap water is genuinely recommended
  • •In Cartagena and coastal areas, default to bottled water
  • •Coffee region towns (Salento, Manizales) vary — ask locally

Recommended Precautions

Use bottled water outside Bogotá, especially on the coast
