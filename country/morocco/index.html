Is Tap Water Safe to Drink in Morocco?

Tap water in major Moroccan cities like Casablanca and Marrakech is chemically treated, but it's generally not recommended for travelers to drink due to high mineral content and gut-sensitivity risk — bottled water is the safer choice.

Water Source

Urban water is treated and chlorinated by ONEE (Office National de l'Eau Potable) in major cities, sourced from dams, rivers, and groundwater.

Contaminants & Concerns

Not typically a major bacterial risk in treated city systems, but high mineral content and different local flora commonly cause stomach upset in travelers. Rural areas have less reliable treatment.

Regional Variations

Casablanca, Rabat, and Marrakech have treated municipal water, though caution is still advised for visitors; rural, desert, and mountain (Atlas) areas carry higher risk and bottled water is essential.

Water Hardness

Hard, commonly 250–400+ mg/L, especially in areas fed by mineral-rich groundwater..

Do Locals Drink Tap Water?

Many Moroccans drink tap water in cities, though bottled water is also very popular and commonly used by visitors.

Tips for Travelers

  • •Bottled water is cheap and sold everywhere
  • •Riads and hotels typically provide bottled water
  • •Avoid ice in casual local eateries outside tourist-oriented establishments

Recommended Precautions

  • •Drink bottled water as a traveler
  • •Avoid tap water and ice in rural and desert regions
